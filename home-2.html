Patient Comfort & Facilities

Designed for Calm, Supportive Visits

Every space inside CardioCare Heart Care Clinic is crafted to make your visit stress-free, private, and accessible.

Ground-Floor Step-Free Entrance

Direct elevator-free access from the parking area, wide automated doors, and complete wheelchair accessibility throughout.

Quiet, Noise-Dampened Waiting Lounge

Peaceful check-in area with acoustic sound-dampening, ergonomic armchair seating, and soothing natural lighting.

Resting ECG & Diagnostic Bay

Private diagnostic bays equipped with modern 12-lead electrocardiography stations and temperature-controlled comfort.

Non-Invasive Ultrasound Suite

Dedicated echocardiography suite offering safe ultrasound assessments with immediate review by your cardiologist.

Family Consultation Rooms

Comfortable consultation suites accommodating family members and caregivers so you can discuss care plans together.

Patient Care Coordinator Desk
